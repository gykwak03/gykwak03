### About Me

I build machine learning systems for health, at the point where clinical data and nutrition science meet. I am an undergraduate at Kyung Hee University with dual degrees in `Food Science & Nutrition` and `Software Convergence`, which lets me read a clinical variable and a model output as the same problem.

As a research intern at the Cardio-Cerebrovascular Center, Severance Hospital, I led a first-author study on **LLM-based feature selection for ICU delirium prediction** over a MIMIC-IV cohort of 32,574 patients. I also spent 2025–26 studying `Data Science and Engineering` at Instituto Superior Técnico in Lisbon.

I am currently preparing for M.S. programs in data science and healthcare AI in the US and Canada.

### Projects

**LLM-Based Delirium Feature Identification** `2024 — 2025` ⭐
First-author study evaluating how large language models prioritize clinical variables for ICU delirium prediction. Compared four LLM feature-selection strategies — Scoring, Ranking, Sequencing, and LMPrior — across GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Haiku, and Grok-3 over 103 literature-derived candidate features from a MIMIC-IV cohort of 32,574 patients, measuring seed consistency, round-number bias, and Spearman/Kendall rank agreement.
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/📄%20Paper-Under%20Review-4568A0?style=flat-square)](https://gykwak03.github.io)

**Seoul Foodservice Site Intelligence** `2026` 🏆
Three-year survival prediction model for foodservice businesses in Seoul, built by merging 16 public datasets. **Grand Prize, 2026 Seoul Big Data Competition.**

**DentiScan** `2026`
Two-stage deep learning pipeline for dental lesion screening, deployed on AWS.

**SS-TI** `Mar — Jul 2024`

**Food Poisoning Battle** `Sep — Oct 2023`

**Book-Delivery Robot Hubs** `Jul — Aug 2023`
Hub placement and route optimization for library book-delivery robots, using nine public datasets.
